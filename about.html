About us

An IT staffing firm built around trust and speed

Kinetix Sphere is an IT staffing and tech workforce augmentation firm based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. We work only in technology.

We connect top-tier tech talent with fast-growing startups and global enterprises through rigorous vetting, rapid deployment and flexible engagement models.

For companies, we add engineering capacity in weeks, not months, with engineers who fit the stack and the team. For engineers, we open doors to roles that match their skills, with honest details and no fees.
