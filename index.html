Hiring models

Pick the engagement that fits your project

Not sure which one you need? Tell us the goal and we will recommend a model.

Contract staffing

Engineers join your team for a fixed period and work to your sprints. Best for project peaks and short or medium assignments.

Contract to hire

Start with a contract and convert the engineer to your payroll after a trial period. Best when you want to test fit first.

Permanent placement

We find, screen and present candidates for full-time roles on your payroll. Best for long-term team members.

Dedicated team

A managed squad with engineers, QA and a lead, working only on your product. Best for ongoing development.

Volume hiring

Leadership and niche search

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How soon can you share profiles?

It depends on the role and skills. We agree an expected timeline for profiles when we confirm your requirement.

Can we interview engineers before we decide?

Yes. You interview and approve every engineer before they start.

What if an engineer is not the right fit?

Replacement terms are written into your agreement, so you are covered if a placement does not work out.

Who handles payroll and compliance for contract engineers?

For contract engagements we can handle payroll and statutory compliance. We confirm the details in your agreement.

Do candidates pay anything?

No. We never charge candidates at any stage.

How is my resume used?

We store it and share it with client companies only for roles that match your profile, as described in our Privacy Policy. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Can I apply without a specific job?

Yes. Use the general application and we will contact you when a matching role opens.

Are roles remote, hybrid or on-site?

It depends on the client. Each role shows its work mode, and the application form lets you tell us your preference.
