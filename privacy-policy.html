This Privacy Policy explains how Kinetix Sphere ("Kinetix Sphere", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you visit our website or contact us about IT staffing and tech workforce augmentation services. It is written with reference to the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India.

1. Who we are

Kinetix Sphere is a proprietorship firm owned by Salma Sanadi, based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Office address: Hosa Road, Opposite Pristine Public School, Bengaluru 560100. Udyam registration number: UDYAM-KR-03-0779420. For privacy matters, contact us at kinetixsphere@gmail.com.

2. Personal data we collect

We collect only the data needed to respond to you and to run our staffing services. This includes:

  • Contact form data: your name, work email address, company name, staffing requirement details, and the message you write.
  • Correspondence: emails, call notes, and messages exchanged with us.
  • Candidate data: if you are a technology professional who shares a resume or profile with us, we collect the details you provide, such as work history, skills, education, contact information, notice period, current and expected pay, and your work-mode and job-type preferences.
  • Technical data: basic information sent by your browser when you visit the website, such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages requested, which is normally recorded in server logs.

3. How we use personal data

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals for staffing requirements.
  • To assess, shortlist, and present candidates to client companies, where the candidate has asked us to do so.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To meet legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.
  • To send service communications related to your request. We send marketing messages only where you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Consent and lawful basis

We process personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit the contact form or send us your details, and for certain legitimate uses permitted by law, such as complying with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to kinetixsphere@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Client companies, for candidates we present to them, with the candidate's knowledge and consent.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as email, hosting, and productivity tools, under confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, courts, regulators, and government authorities where we are legally required to do so.

6. International transfers

Because we work with global companies, personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside India. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the data is handled with appropriate safeguards and in line with applicable law.

7. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy or as required by law. Enquiry data is kept while the business relationship or discussion is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. Candidate data is kept while the candidate is actively seeking opportunities, and is deleted or anonymised on request. In practice, we keep enquiry data and candidate data for up to 24 months after your last interaction with us, unless a longer period is required by law or you ask us to delete it sooner.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data, or to update it.
  • Ask us to erase personal data that is no longer needed.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and, if it is not resolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, write to kinetixsphere@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Cookies and third-party fonts

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for it to work. If we add analytics or other optional cookies, we will describe them here and ask for your consent where required. You can control cookies through your browser settings. This website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch the font files.

11. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and working professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-party links

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their policies.
