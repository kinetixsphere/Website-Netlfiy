How hiring works for candidates

  1. ApplyPick a role or send a general application with your resume.
  2. Screening callA recruiter talks to you about skills, expectations and notice period.
  3. Technical roundA short skills discussion or assessment for the role.
  4. Client interviewYou meet the hiring team. We keep you updated at each stage.
  5. Offer and joiningOffer, documents and onboarding support.
Fraud alert. Kinetix Sphere never asks candidates for money, a registration fee, or bank or card details. Be careful with job offers on messaging apps. Official emails come only from our own website domain.
Questions

Candidate FAQ

Do candidates pay anything?

No. We never charge candidates at any stage.

How is my resume used?

We store it and share it with client companies only for roles that match your profile, as described in our Privacy Policy. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Can I apply without a specific job?

Yes. Use the general application and we will contact you when a matching role opens.

Are roles remote, hybrid or on-site?

It depends on the client. Each role shows its work mode, and the application form lets you tell us your preference.

Where can I see new roles?

New openings are posted on the Daily Openings page, and on our LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook pages.
